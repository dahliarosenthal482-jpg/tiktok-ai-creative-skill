## Available Data

### Hook Patterns

- Low-effort/easy-workout opening.
- Morning-routine or daily-habit opening.
- Skepticism-to-surprise: “Thought this was a scam...”
- Immediate benefit framing: “Vibration plate benefits”.
- First-use physical reaction and unexpected vibration intensity.
- Instructional framing around foot placement or stance.
- Time-pain framing: no time for the gym / short home routine.

Evidence: Existing Kalodata summary for Merach Slim and HOPHORSE 3-in-1. Confidence: Medium because the raw video URLs are not retained.

### Scene Patterns

- Person and product appear at the beginning.
- Start/control-panel interaction followed by visible vibration reaction.
- Short standing demonstration, stance change or squat variation.
- Home, morning-routine and low-effort workout contexts.
- Longer formats add first-use narration, experience explanation or instructional demonstration.

Evidence: Existing Kalodata analysis summary. Confidence: Low–Medium; no timestamped per-video scene records are available.

### CTA Patterns

- TikTok Shop deal/current-price CTA.
- Limited-time offer language.
- Save the routine / shop below.
- Select a product color or configuration.
- Included-accessory/value CTA.

Evidence: Existing derived analysis and creative-planning notes. These are pattern summaries, not verified per-video CTA records. Confidence: Low–Medium.

### Common Selling Angles

- Easy home workout and routine consistency.
- Low setup burden and no-gym convenience.
- Strong first-use sensation/reaction.
- Beginner-friendly stance or movement demonstration.
- Multiple movements with resistance bands.
- Compact home-fitness use.

High-risk competitor language observed in the prior analysis includes deterministic weight loss, lymphatic drainage, pain relief, bowel/“detox” effects and automatic fat-burning claims. These are not approved HTM claims.
