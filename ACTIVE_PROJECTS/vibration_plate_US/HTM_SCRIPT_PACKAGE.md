## 2. Script Objective

### Primary objective: Consideration

- Viewer Goal: understand one clear HTM home-fitness role and evaluate whether the approved product value fits the viewer's needs.
- Business Goal: support a qualified progression from product understanding to consideration without outcome guarantees.
- Measurement Direction: product-detail interaction, saves/questions when available, and add-to-cart signals; these metrics do not establish creative causality by themselves.

### Supporting objective: Conversion Support

- Viewer Goal: reduce uncertainty around exact product identity, included value, expectations and available choice.
- Business Goal: support a compliant purchase decision using verified facts and current information only.
- Measurement Direction: verified clicks, orders, conversion rate and GMV, separated from attention metrics and paid-distribution effects.

Awareness may serve as an entry function, but it is not allowed to displace product understanding or trust.
