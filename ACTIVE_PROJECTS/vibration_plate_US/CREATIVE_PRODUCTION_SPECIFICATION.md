# 2. Creative Objective

Translate the approved Strategy into a clear reason for producing content:

- Reduce the audience's decision, understanding and starting barriers without making outcome guarantees.
- Make approved product value and intended home-fitness role easier to evaluate.
- Preserve restrained trust, exact product identity and factual claim boundaries.

Evidence classification: `Inference` derived from the approved Creative Strategy. It is not a verified performance rule.

# 3. Audience Direction

Audience direction: US home-fitness shoppers evaluating an understandable and manageable category entry.

Permitted audience needs include decision clarity, product understanding, home-routine relevance and trust. Space, consistency, settings, sound, comfort and outcome uncertainty remain source-bounded Customer Insight, not Product Truth or universal behavior.

This layer defines direction only. It does not write audience copy, target protected characteristics, create a character profile or prescribe a Scene.

# 4. Business Objective

Business objective: support a traceable progression from qualified attention to product understanding, consideration and a compliant purchase decision.

Required measurement directions:

- Attention: qualified viewing signals when available.
- Understanding: watch progression and product-detail interaction.
- Consideration: saves, questions or add-to-cart signals when available.
- Action: verified commerce metrics.

Attention and purchase metrics remain separate. Views alone cannot establish commercial success or creative causality.

# 5. Content Goal

The content should help the audience:

- Recognize a relevant home-fitness decision context.
- Understand one approved product role or factual value at a time.
- Evaluate proof and expectations within approved evidence boundaries.
- Identify an appropriate next decision step without false urgency.

This section defines communication goals, not Information Flow, Hook selection, wording, duration or production treatment.

# 6. Production Intent

Production Intent defines what the future content must accomplish conceptually:

- Communicate one primary objective per content unit unless a reviewed package permits supporting objectives.
- Preserve Product Truth, approved claims, audience direction and evidence classification.
- Make the chosen approved value or role understandable without specification overload.
- Include an appropriate trust or proof requirement when the decision stage requires it.
- Avoid premium parity, medical framing, guaranteed results, fabricated proof and unsupported urgency.

Production Intent does not define how information is sequenced or how AI generation is executed.

# 7. Content Success Direction

|Success Dimension|Direction|Boundary|
|---|---|---|
|Relevance|Audience recognizes the approved need or decision context|No invented pain point or universalization|
|Comprehension|Audience can understand the selected approved role or value|No unapproved feature or instruction|
|Trust|Evidence and limitations remain traceable and restrained|No fabricated social proof or guarantee|
|Consideration|Audience can evaluate fit and next step|No pressure, false scarcity or stale offer|
|Learning|Results are evaluated by objective-appropriate metrics|No single asset or View count establishes a winning rule|

# 8. Product and Claim Boundary References

- Product Truth, Selling SKU, Visual Lock and Approved Claims remain governed exclusively by `PRODUCT_PRODUCTION_READY.md` and controlling product records.
- Black and White remain the referenced production variants; Silver remains prohibited.
- Customer Insight may inform audience direction but cannot become Product Fact.
- Video Intelligence remains `PARTIAL COMPLETE`, Quality A = 0; no Hook, Scene, character, Camera style, duration or CTA is proven.
- Time-sensitive offer information requires reverification in the applicable downstream instance.

This layer references boundaries but does not define Product Lock implementation or generation constraints.

Creative Production Specification must not contain:

- Script wording, spoken copy, Hook sentence, advertising copy or CTA copy.
- Information Flow, Message Flow, Storyboard, Shot List or Scene timeline.

- Visual Style, Camera, Scene, Motion or Character production requirement.
- Provider, Adapter, Model or compatibility selection.
- Prompt, generation parameter, API payload or Generation Task.
