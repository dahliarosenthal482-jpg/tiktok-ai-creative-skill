# 11. Generation Constraint

## Mandatory controls

- Product Fidelity: use the approved Black or White reference; reject geometry, control, display, logo, scale, packaging or accessory drift.
- Claim Compliance: use approved factual, non-guaranteed claims only. Customer Insight is not product proof.
- Platform Style Fit: TikTok-native UGC conventions are permitted, but comments, testimonials, engagement and organic-performance claims cannot be fabricated.
- Source Permission: competitor evidence is read-only intelligence. Do not copy footage, likeness, captions, audio or scene sequences.
- Offer Accuracy: `$49.99` cannot appear unless reverified immediately before publication.
- Evidence Labeling: preserve `Observed Evidence`, `Inference` and `Future Test Direction`.

## Negative constraints

- No Silver or mixed Black/White unit.
- No medical, treatment, rehabilitation, pain, circulation, lymphatic or disease claim.
- No automatic fat burning, guaranteed weight loss, transformation, comfort, quietness, stability, safety or result.
- No unverified capacity, wattage, motor, dimensions, remote, Bluetooth, speaker, app, battery, contents, warranty, eligibility, superiority or premium parity.
- No body shaming, before/after transformation, unsafe placement or impossible interaction.
- No claim that a Hook, scene, character, camera style, duration or CTA is already effective.
