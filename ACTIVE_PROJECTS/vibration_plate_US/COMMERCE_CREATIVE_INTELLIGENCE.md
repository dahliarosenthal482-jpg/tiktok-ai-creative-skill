Purpose: Explain which content and offer logics may strengthen purchase consideration for HTM. This report does not claim which image, scene, hook, action or duration caused a sale.

## Decision Summary

The strongest defensible HTM position is not “a cheaper premium machine” and not “automatic results.” It is a lower-commitment way to begin a clearly explained home-movement routine: temporary entry price after reverification, compact Black/White product, included Resistance Bands, understandable controls and realistic expectations.

The commerce evidence supports a sequence of business questions rather than a fixed script:

1. Attention: can the viewer immediately recognize a relevant home-exercise problem or value gap?
2. Interest: can the product be understood without technical overload?
3. Desire: can the viewer imagine a repeatable five-minute routine and see what is included?
4. Action: is the current offer factual, low-friction and trustworthy enough to click?

Every proposed implementation below is a Future Test Direction.

## Evidence Base

### Observed Evidence

- Five Commerce Validation records have positive video-specific GMV and Orders, associated Product IDs, Views, dates and Quality B status.
- The five commerce records total approximately ¥191.3K displayed GMV, 239 Orders and 638.0K Views. Currency is retained as displayed.
- All five commerce records are labeled AD. Paid distribution is therefore a material confounder.
- The commerce captions encode distinct purchase propositions: direct product/power identity, hybrid function, color/novelty, upgrade framing and limited-time sale.
- Displayed GMV per thousand Views varies materially across the five records, from about ¥120 to ¥2,077. Reach is not a proxy for purchase efficiency.
- Existing 30-day market data shows strongly video-led links at both premium/core and near-$50 price levels. HOPHORSE ranks 9/12/13 and ROTAI rank 15 are the closest value-band channel evidence.
- Amazon parent-family customer signals include adjustable settings 6/13, easy routine/consistency 5/13, compact storage 4/13, sound complaints 3/13, platform discomfort 1/13 and bands variety 1/13.
- Approved HTM facts include Black/White SKUs, compact vibration-plate form, 120 listed levels, 5 programs, LED/push-button controls and included Resistance Bands. The $49.99 price is temporary and must be reverified.

### Inference

- A clear proposition may help viewers decide whether the product fits them, but the evidence does not show which caption phrase or creative element caused conversion.
- Lower decision cost may come from combining a familiar category, an entry offer, a short routine and visible included value.
- Trust may be improved by restrained claims and direct handling of realistic objections, but this remains untested for HTM.

### Future Test Direction

- Test attention, comprehension, desire, trust, objection removal and CTA separately rather than treating Views as a conversion result.
- Measure hold rate/Views for Attention, product-page click for Interest, add-to-cart for Desire and Orders/GMV for Action.
- Do not promote any hypothesis to “proven” until repeated positive performance exists across multiple HTM creatives.

# Module 3 — Competitive Content Positioning

## Premium Competitors — Market Education → HTM Opportunity

Observed Market Education:

- Premium Merach/LifePro/ANCHEER offers teach that home vibration equipment can justify consideration through brand, technical differentiation, reassurance, hybrid hardware or live explanation.
- Merach has large observed GMV across multiple price points, but some premium links are live-dependent and do not establish short-video replication value.

HTM Opportunity — Inference:

- Accept the category legitimacy already created by premium players.
- Do not claim premium parity. Reframe the decision as a simpler, lower-commitment starting point centered on an approved home routine, included bands and exact product proof.

Risk:

- Never borrow high-power, capacity, 4D, treadmill, FSA/HSA, warranty or premium-brand claims without HTM-specific proof.

## Core Competitors — Market Education → HTM Opportunity

Observed Market Education:

- Core Merach and RELIFE offers normalize compact/slim vibration plates as mainstream home-fitness choices.
- Merach Slim rank 1 is strongly video-led at about 87.9% of product-link GMV, supporting short-form channel relevance without proving creative cause.

HTM Opportunity — Inference:

- Reduce explanation cost: one routine role, one value proposition and a simpler Black/White decision.
- Use verified controls and bands to make utility tangible without a specification contest.

Risk:

- Do not claim equivalent build quality, capacity, motor power or category leadership.

## Entry Competitors — Market Education → HTM Opportunity

Observed Market Education:

- HOPHORSE $46.79–$53.55 links and ROTAI $47.31 link show that near-$50 products can generate substantial units and video-led GMV.
- HOPHORSE ranks 12/13 retain 891/791 units; ROTAI rank 15 retains 603 units. Video shares are approximately 94.3%, 90.3% and 90.5% respectively.

HTM Opportunity — Inference:

- Avoid a pure price race. Make the offer easier to understand through a five-minute routine, included Resistance Bands, exact Black/White choice and honest controls.
- Convert existing entry-band interest into a more coherent use case rather than an unsupported superiority comparison.

Risk:

- Health-adjacent lymphatic, pain, detox, fat-loss and body-transformation language cannot be transferred.
