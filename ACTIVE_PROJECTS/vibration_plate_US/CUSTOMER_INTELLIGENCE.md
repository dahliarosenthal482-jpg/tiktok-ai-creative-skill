# Usage Scenario

- Short home sessions as part of a daily or near-daily routine.
- Standing on the plate while watching television.
- Use on low-energy days when a treadmill or conventional workout feels too demanding.
- Storage under a bed or in a closet after use.
- Use in small apartments or while other household members are present.
- Wearing sneakers or thick socks for platform comfort.
- Adjusting foot placement to reduce knocking/clicking reported by some reviewers.

Evidence Sources: Amazon visible verified-purchase reviews. Safety, duration, and suitability are not validated instructions.
