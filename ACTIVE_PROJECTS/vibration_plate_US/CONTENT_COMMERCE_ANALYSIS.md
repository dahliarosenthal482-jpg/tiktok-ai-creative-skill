## Why Audiences Watch vs Why They Buy

### Playback reasons

- Unexpected vibration or physical reaction.
- A low-effort or “can I fit this into my day?” premise.
- Immediate transformation of an ordinary home moment into a routine.
- Skepticism-to-surprise or first-use curiosity.

### Purchase reasons

- Price fits an entry-level decision.
- Product appears simple enough to use and store.
- Viewer can picture a repeatable home routine.
- Included Resistance Bands expand perceived use without a second purchase.
- Trust is established through real controls, consistent product visuals and restrained claims.

## Trigger Analysis

### Attention Trigger

Recommended test: show the correct Black or White HTM in motion in the first shot and state a time-constrained routine premise. Evidence is a retained competitor-pattern summary, not a verified winning first frame.

### Desire Trigger

Translate the machine into an attainable habit: “my 5-minute home movement routine.” Customer evidence supports easy routine and compact storage as recurring motivations. Do not translate desire into promised fat loss or medical benefit.

### Trust Trigger

- Demonstrate the LED/push-button controls and a real stance/movement.
- Show Resistance Bands only as included for the target SKU.
- Preserve Black/White visual lock.
- Use customer concerns to make the demo more credible: realistic sound, stable placement and comfortable footwear as an optional customer-described choice.

### Purchase Trigger

Use the temporary $49.99 price only after publication-time reverification. The relevant comparison is the observed entry band, especially HOPHORSE/ROTAI/GetMod, not a fabricated regular price or savings claim.

### Objection Removal

- “Will I use it?” → show a short, repeatable routine.
- “Is it complicated?” → show the control sequence without claiming universal ease.
- “What can I do with it?” → demonstrate standing movement plus included bands.
- “Is it noisy/uncomfortable?” → show normal-use audio honestly and optional shoes/thick socks; do not promise silence or give medical advice.
- “Will it guarantee results?” → explicitly frame it as a routine tool, not an automatic outcome machine.

### CTA Trigger

Test a factual CTA sequence: current deal → Black/White selection → Resistance Bands included → shop link. Limited-time language must reflect a verified live offer.
