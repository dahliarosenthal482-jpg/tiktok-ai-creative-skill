## Strategic Decision

HTM should be understood as an accessible way to begin a clear, compact home-movement routine—not as a cheaper copy of a premium machine and not as an automatic-results device.

The strategic promise is a reduction of three barriers:

1. Purchase barrier: a lower-commitment entry position, with temporary price used only after reverification.
2. Understanding barrier: one clear product role, factual controls and included Resistance Bands.
3. Usage barrier: a defined five-minute home-routine concept that makes starting easier to imagine without promising outcomes.

## Evidence Language

- `Observed Evidence`: directly supported by approved Product Truth, commerce/market records or classified Customer Insight.
- `Inference`: strategic interpretation of those signals; not proven creative causality.
- `Future Test`: a hypothesis to evaluate with HTM-specific content and performance data.

No structure, Hook category, angle, role or emotional trigger in this framework is described as guaranteed to increase Views or conversion.

## 2. Positioning Architecture

### Observed Evidence

- HTM approved facts: slim compact form, Black/White, 120 listed levels, 5 programs, LED/push-button controls and Resistance Bands included.
- Temporary $49.99 places HTM near observed entry competitors but must be reverified before use.
- Customer Insight signals: settings 6/13, easy routine 5/13, compact/storage 4/13, sound concerns 3/13 and comfort 1/13.
- Commerce Creative Intelligence found that reach and GMV efficiency diverge and that all five commerce samples are AD.

### Inference

HTM can be differentiated through decision clarity rather than a price-only or feature-parity claim. The consumer should be able to answer three questions quickly:

- What role could this product have in my home routine?
- What factual value is included?
- Why is this a manageable purchase decision now?

### Future Test

Test the positioning territory:

`Familiar home-fitness category → clear five-minute role → included value → lower-commitment purchase`

This is a strategy hypothesis, not finished marketing copy.

## 3. Positioning Guardrails

- Say “accessible entry” through factual offer and simplicity; do not say or imply “same as premium for less.”
- Define value using approved HTM facts; do not win by attacking competitor quality.
- Define “beginner-friendly” as lower explanation and starting friction; do not guarantee safety, suitability or ease for every user.
- Use customer concerns to guide questions and transparency; do not convert them into universal defects or Product Facts.

# Module 4 — Video Structure Framework

All structure models are `Future Test Direction`. They are abstract information sequences, not shot lists or execution timelines.

## 1. Concise Commerce Model — approximately 15 seconds

`Attention → Need Recognition → Product Understanding → One Value Proof → Action`

Strategic use: one simple proposition, low explanation burden and one factual purchase reason.

Primary risks: rushing trust, price-first commoditization and unsupported visual causality.

## 2. Standard Commerce Model — approximately 30 seconds

`Problem / Aspiration → Product Role → Value Explanation → Objection Reduction → Verified Offer → Action`

Strategic use: balance attention with enough comprehension and trust to support a product-page click.

Primary risks: too many claims, specification overload and unverified scene assumptions.

## 3. Education / FAQ Model — approximately 30–45 seconds

`Question → Category Clarification → Approved Product Facts → Expectation Boundary → Decision Guidance → Action`

Strategic use: address high-intent uncertainty about controls, included value, category role or purchase fit.

Primary risks: fabricated comments, unverified instructions and turning Customer Insight into Product Fact.

## 4. Objection Resolution Model — approximately 30–60 seconds

`Concern → Evidence Boundary → Factual Product Context → Honest Limitation → Purchase-Fit Decision`

Strategic use: build trust for shoppers concerned about complexity, expectations, sound or comfort.

Primary risks: universalizing a small review sample, implying defects or giving unsupported safety/medical guidance.

## 5. Routine-Series Model — variable length

`Recurring Context → One Routine Theme → One Product Role → Continuity Cue → Next Evaluation Point`

Strategic use: test whether repeatable content organization improves recognition or return viewing.

Primary risks: presenting a challenge as guaranteed transformation, inventing outcomes or declaring retention before measurement.

## Structure Testing Rules

- Duration is a variable, not a conclusion; commerce samples range from 14s to 122s.
- Test one strategic difference at a time where possible: angle, Hook category, proof type, objection or CTA role.
- Structure success requires stage-appropriate metrics, not Views alone.
- No structure may be translated into a shot timeline without a separately approved production task.

## Playback Metrics vs Purchase Metrics

- Playback metrics diagnose attention and information delivery.
- Purchase metrics diagnose commerce response.
- A high-view asset can have weak commerce efficiency; a lower-view asset can have stronger displayed GMV per thousand Views.
- No asset should be selected as a “winner” using Views alone.

# Module 6 — AI Production Preparation

This module defines future production controls only. It contains no generation prompt.

## 1. Product Fidelity

- Use only the approved Black or White reference for a production asset.
- Preserve the slim oval form, surface pattern, rim, base, proportions, feet, openings, logo placement, control panel, button/icon arrangement, display and band attachment structure.
- Do not invent capacity, motor, remote, Bluetooth, speaker, app control, battery or accessories.

## 2. Visual Lock

- Never show Silver.
- Never combine Black and White into a mixed-color unit.
- Keep SKU color, structure and approved accessory relationship consistent throughout one asset.
- Reject outputs with geometry drift, moving controls, altered display, changed logo placement or invented attachment points.

## 3. Role Fidelity

- A future character role must serve the selected customer need and content angle, not imitate a competitor creator.
- Do not claim that a particular age, gender, body type, family role or creator archetype improves conversion.
- Maintain identity, wardrobe and behavior continuity if a person appears.
- Avoid protected-characteristic targeting, body shaming or implied medical suitability.

## 4. Scene Consistency

- A future scene must support the strategic product role and remain physically plausible.
- Product scale, floor contact, band attachment, lighting direction and room continuity must remain stable.
- Do not show unverified storage fit, unsafe placement or product behavior.
- Scene selection remains a future production decision; this framework does not prescribe individual shots.

## 5. Claim Compliance

- Use approved factual claims only and non-guaranteed wording.
- Reverify $49.99 immediately before publication.
- Reject automatic fat loss, guaranteed transformation, medical/treatment, circulation, lymphatic, rehabilitation, pain-relief or guaranteed quietness/comfort/stability/safety/results claims.
- Customer Insight may inspire questions and objections but cannot become product proof.

## 6. Accessory Fidelity

- Resistance Bands may be shown as included.
- Do not invent a remote or other package contents for the TikTok SKU.
- Do not imply that bands create a competitor-defined “3-in-1” product unless separately verified.

## 7. Production QA Gates

|Gate|Pass Condition|Reject Condition|
|---|---|---|
|Product Truth|Every stated feature is in Product Production Ready|Amazon-only or competitor feature imported|
|SKU / Visual Lock|One approved color and exact product identity remain consistent|Silver, mixed color or geometry/control drift|
|Role Fidelity|Character serves the hypothesis without identity or outcome claims|Body shaming, medical targeting or claimed archetype causality|
|Scene Consistency|Physical relationships and product scale remain plausible|Floating product, changing room/product or impossible band attachment|
|Claim Compliance|Only approved factual, non-guaranteed claims|Health, fat-loss, guaranteed result or unsupported superiority claim|
|Offer Accuracy|Current price and availability reverified|Expired price, invented saving or false urgency|
|Evidence Label|Observed, Inference and Future Test remain distinguishable|Hypothesis presented as proven creative fact|
