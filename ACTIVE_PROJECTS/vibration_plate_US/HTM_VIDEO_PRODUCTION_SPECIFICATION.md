## 4. Style Requirement

### UGC Native Video Style

Required direction:

- Real user-generated-content feeling.
- Consumer-created appearance rather than polished commercial advertising.
- Believable everyday context and restrained presentation.
- Clear product identity without a glossy studio-sales aesthetic.

Avoid:

- Commercial studio look.
- Luxury campaign treatment that implies premium-equipment equivalence.
- Over-produced lighting, artificial perfection or synthetic testimonial behavior.
- Visual treatment that hides or changes the approved product.

### Smartphone Camera Reference Style

Required direction:

- Modern smartphone rear-camera visual character as a reference style, without naming or locking a specific device model.
- Natural lighting behavior.
- Realistic skin texture when a person is authorized.
- Natural handheld feeling where compatible with product visibility and stability.
- Authentic environment and consumer-created appearance.
- Realistic exposure and autofocus behavior without intentional product obscuration.

UGC Native Video Style and Smartphone Camera Reference Style are `Future Test Direction`. They are production choices, not validated winning patterns.

## 5. Camera Requirement

Camera requirements are functional, not numerical parameters:

- Handheld smartphone perspective where appropriate to the selected Content Type.
- Natural framing that keeps the product and relevant interaction understandable.
- Realistic autofocus behavior; temporary focus transition is acceptable only when product identity returns to clear visibility.
- Consumer-video feeling with plausible operator movement.
- Stable enough presentation whenever approved controls, product structure, accessory relationship or color choice supplies proof.
- No impossible camera motion, artificial floating viewpoint or geometry-changing distortion.

No lens, focal-length, frame-rate, exposure, shutter, ISO, resolution, device-model or provider parameter is specified.

## 8. Scene Requirement

Scene requirements define environment constraints, not a Storyboard or Shot List.

- Realistic home environment appropriate to an approved home-fitness context.
- Everyday environment with plausible floor, lighting, space and object relationships.
- Product visibility sufficient to verify color, identity and selected proof function.
- Appropriate usage context consistent with approved Production Intent and Product Truth.
- Room, lighting, floor and product continuity must remain stable within an asset.
- No unverified storage fit, unsafe placement, clinical/medical setting implication or commercial studio set.
- No fabricated review, comment, testimonial, engagement interface or before/after context.

No Scene sequence, Scene count, Scene duration or Scene timeline is specified because verified Scene Timeline Evidence is missing.

## 9. Motion Requirement

- Natural human movement when a role is authorized.
- Realistic product interaction consistent with approved usage boundaries.
- Plausible posture and weight relationship without implied medical suitability.
- Stable product geometry during motion.
- Realistic band attachment and movement when Resistance Bands are shown.
- Avoid product deformation, floating, sliding without cause, impossible vibration, limb distortion, body transformation and inconsistent physical contact.
- Avoid exaggerated reactions that imply guaranteed intensity, therapeutic effect or transformation.

No motion pattern is treated as proven to improve retention or conversion.
